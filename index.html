Hi, I'm Ananya! I’m a Human-Centered Computing PhD student at Georgia Tech, working with the D3 Lab and advised by Dr. Naveena Karusala. My research is at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction, labor, and policy. I use qualitative methods and community-engaged approaches to understand the experiences of women gig workers and to provide policy recommendations focused on more dignified futures. Previously, I graduated from the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, where I worked with the Computational HCI lab advised by Dr. Nikola Banovic.
