Hi, I am Ananya. I am a PhD student in Human-Centered Computing at Georgia Tech. I work with the D3 Lab advised by Dr. Naveena Karusala. My research is at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction, labor, and policy. I use qualitative methods and community-engaged approaches to understand the experiences of women gig workers and to provide policy recommendations focused on more dignified futures.  

Previously, I graduated from the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, where I worked with the Computational HCI Lab advised by Dr. Nikola Banovic.
