PhD Student in Human-Centered Computing, Georgia Tech

I research the design and societal impacts of technology in the world of work, with a focus on gig labor, platform work, and policy—particularly the experiences of women gig workers. I use qualitative methods and community-engaged approaches to understand their lived experiences and to inform more equitable and dignified futures.

I work with the D3 Lab at Georgia Tech, advised by Dr. Naveena Karusala. Previously, I graduated from the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, where I worked with the Computational HCI Lab advised by Dr. Nikola Banovic.
